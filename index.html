What we are

A mentorship pipeline built around one exam.

Chemistry Competition Prep was established by the IChC Local Ambassador for Ghana to identify chemistry-talented students, pair them with dedicated coordinators, and take them through structured, ambassador-led preparation for the International Chemistry Competition — an event where students from around the world are tested on the same field.

Why this exists

Talent isn't the scarce resource. Access is.

Plenty of Ghanaian students could hold their own on an international chemistry stage — what they usually lack isn't ability, it's exposure: practice material written to competition standard, a mentor who knows what the exam actually asks of you, and a community of peers chasing the same goal.

Chemistry Competition Prep exists to close that gap directly — established and led by the IChC Local Ambassador for Ghana, it offers not a one-off workshop, but a sustained, term-long relationship between each student and a coordinator invested in training them toward the competition.

The problem: competition-grade preparation is concentrated in a handful of well-resourced schools.
Our approach: recruit broadly, mentor closely, and measure progress honestly.
The result: a growing cohort of students who know they belong on that stage.
About the competition

The International Chemistry Competition

The International Chemistry Competition (IChC) is an independent online competition for middle school, high school and university students from any country. Everyone solves the same problems across three rounds, and all you need is a pen, paper and an internet connection.

Its problems draw on recently published research, cover the main fields of chemistry, and scale in difficulty, so it works as a first step into real chemistry beyond the school curriculum.

How the programme works

From application to the international stage.

1

Apply

Complete the short application form — no prior competition experience required, just genuine interest and a foundation in chemistry.

2

Interview

Every application is reviewed. Shortlisted applicants are invited to a short, friendly interview with the Ambassador and coordinators. The date, time and details arrive by email and text.

3

Onboard

Accepted students get their portal, confirm their place and watch a short orientation video. That unlocks the roadmap and daily practice.

4

Get matched

You meet your coordinator, placed with you by region and availability, and join the official WhatsApp group.

5

Train weekly

Regular call sessions, fresh practice questions, and shared study materials — all tracked in one place.

6

Sit mock rounds

Timed practice competitions that build exam stamina and reveal exactly where to focus next.

7

Compete

The strongest-prepared students go forward to represent Ghana at the International Chemistry Competition.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the International Chemistry Competition?

An international competition where students are tested on chemistry knowledge and problem-solving against peers from around the world. Chemistry Competition Prep was created by the IChC Local Ambassador for Ghana to train Ghanaian students for it.

Who can apply?

Any student with a genuine interest in chemistry and a willingness to commit to weekly sessions. Prior competition experience isn't required.

Is the programme free?

Yes — the programme is run by volunteer coordinators and an ambassador, and does not charge students to participate.

How much time does it take each week?

Typically one structured call session plus a practice question to work through independently — coordinators share exact scheduling with their group.

How do I apply?

Use the JOIN PROGRAMME NOW button on this page to open the application form. It takes about five minutes.

What happens after I apply?

First we email you to confirm we received your application. Then we review it. If you are shortlisted, we invite you to a short interview and send the date, time and details by email and text message. After the interview we let you know whether you are accepted.

If you are accepted, your student portal is switched on and you are guided through onboarding: confirm your place, watch a short orientation video (which unlocks your roadmap and daily practice), meet your coordinator and join the official WhatsApp group.

Is there an interview?

Yes. Shortlisted applicants have a short, friendly conversation with the Ambassador and some of the coordinators, online, by phone or in person. It is a chance for us to get to know you and for you to ask your questions. Check your email and text messages (and your spam folder) for the details.

I was accepted — how do I access the portal?

Your student portal is switched on when you are accepted after your interview. Being invited to an interview does not switch it on. Sign in with the Google account for the email you applied with. If it doesn't work after you have been accepted, email the Ambassador and they'll check your registration.

For parents & teachers

A note before you say yes.

Chemistry Competition Prep is an independent, volunteer-run initiative founded by the IChC Local Ambassador for Ghana — not a paid service, and not the International Chemistry Competition itself. It's led by that Ambassador and a team of coordinators who volunteer their time to train students toward the competition. Communication happens directly between coordinators and students for scheduling and practice material, and parents and teachers are welcome to reach the Ambassador directly with any questions before or during a student's participation.

Privacy Policy

What we collect: when you apply or are added to the programme, we collect your name, email, and (optionally) phone number to place you with a coordinator and send you programme updates.

How we use it: solely to run the programme — matching you with a coordinator, sharing call sheets and materials, and sending announcements by email or SMS if you opt in.

Who sees it: your coordinator, the Local Ambassador, IChC, and the service providers listed below, which only process it to run the programme for us. No one else sees it. We do not sell your information.

For students under 18: if you're applying as a minor, we recommend a parent or guardian be aware of your participation and have visibility into your coordinator's contact details. Parents or guardians can reach the Ambassador at any time with questions about how their child's information is handled.

Services we use: you sign in to the portal with your Google account, and Google shares your name and email address with us (never your password). Your information is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the website is hosted on Vercel. We send programme updates by email and text message through our messaging providers, and, if you allow them, by browser or phone push notifications through OneSignal. The application form may use Google reCAPTCHA to block spam.

Chemistry Series sign-up: if you register for the Chemistry Series, we collect your full name, email address, school or university, your role, the days and times that suit you, and, if you choose to give it, your WhatsApp number. Depending on how you join, we also keep what you tell us: your preferred session and question, a topic you suggest and why, or your speaker details (field, talk title, short bio and link). We use this only to run the series: to confirm your registration, send session details and reminders, plan topics and speakers, and contact you about the series, as you agreed when you ticked the consent box. Only the Local Ambassador and the Series Manager appointed for the series can see these responses, and the Series Manager signs in with a one-time code sent to their email or with their Google account. Your confirmation is sent by email. We do not sell this information, and you can ask for your sign-up to be removed at any time.

Website messages, newsletter and visits: if you send us a message, report a problem or ask to speak to a person from the chat, we keep your name (if you give it), your email address, your message, and the page and type of device you were using, so we can reply and fix problems. If you subscribe to the newsletter we keep your email address and first name (if you give it) until you unsubscribe, which you can do from any newsletter. We also count visits to this website without cookies, without your IP address and without any way to identify you: we record only a random code for your browser session, the section you viewed, your device type and the website you came from. This counting is switched off if your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Your choices: you can opt out of email or SMS notifications at any time, and can ask the Ambassador to remove your record from the programme at any time by emailing eseyram@amb.intchc.org.

You can switch off push notifications at any time in your browser or phone settings. Last updated: 9 November 2026.
