About the competition

The International Chemistry Competition

The International Chemistry Competition (IChC) is an independent online competition for middle school, high school and university students from any country. Everyone solves the same problems across three rounds, and all you need is a pen, paper and an internet connection.

Its problems draw on recently published research, cover the main fields of chemistry, and scale in difficulty, so it works as a first step into real chemistry beyond the school curriculum.

Why this exists

Talent isn't the scarce resource. Access is.

Plenty of Ghanaian students could hold their own on an international chemistry stage — what they usually lack isn't ability, it's exposure: practice material written to competition standard, a mentor who knows what the exam actually asks of you, and a community of peers chasing the same goal.

Chemistry Competition Prep exists to close that gap directly — established and led by Local Ambassador, IChC, it offers not a one-off workshop, but a sustained, term-long relationship between each student and a coordinator invested in training them toward the competition.

The problem: competition-grade preparation is concentrated in a handful of well-resourced schools.
Our approach: recruit broadly, mentor closely, and measure progress honestly.
The result: a growing cohort of students who know they belong on that stage.
What students gain

More than exam prep.

Every pillar of the programme is designed to compound — instincts sharpened weekly, feedback that's actually personal, and a track record you can point to.

How the programme works

From application to the international stage.

1

Apply

Complete the short application form — no prior competition experience required, just genuine interest and a foundation in chemistry.

2

Get matched

Accepted students are placed into a group with a coordinator, based on region and availability.

3

Train weekly

Regular call sessions, fresh practice questions, and shared study materials — all tracked in one place.

4

Sit mock rounds

Timed practice competitions that build exam stamina and reveal exactly where to focus next.

5

Compete

The strongest-prepared students go forward to represent Ghana at the International Chemistry Competition.

A note to every student

Someone had to ask the first question. It can be you.

The bread on your table, the medicine that once healed you, the clean water in your glass and the phone in your hand all began with a person in a room who was curious and refused to stop. Chemistry is not reserved for the gifted. It is patience, honest mistakes, and the courage to try again until the result finally makes sense.

You do not need to have started early, to come from a famous school, or to be the fastest in the room. If you are afraid of getting it wrong, you are in good company: every chemist whose name you know began exactly there. Here, your questions are welcome, your progress is seen, and you are never learning alone.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the International Chemistry Competition?

An international competition where students are tested on chemistry knowledge and problem-solving against peers from around the world. Chemistry Competition Prep was created by Local Ambassador, IChC to train Ghanaian students for it.

Who can apply?

Any student with a genuine interest in chemistry and a willingness to commit to weekly sessions. Prior competition experience isn't required.

Is the programme free?

Yes — the programme is run by volunteer coordinators and an ambassador, and does not charge students to participate.

How much time does it take each week?

Typically one structured call session plus a practice question to work through independently — coordinators share exact scheduling with their group.

How do I apply?

Use the Apply link on this page to complete the application form. Accepted students are contacted by email with next steps and matched with a coordinator.

I was accepted — how do I access the portal?

Sign in to the portal using the exact name and email your coordinator or the Ambassador registered you with.

For parents & teachers

A note before you say yes.

Chemistry Competition Prep is an independent, volunteer-run initiative founded by Local Ambassador, IChC — not a paid service, and not the International Chemistry Competition itself. It's led by that Ambassador and a team of coordinators who volunteer their time to train students toward the competition. Communication happens directly between coordinators and students for scheduling and practice material, and parents and teachers are welcome to reach the Ambassador directly with any questions before or during a student's participation.

Privacy Policy

What we collect: when you apply or are added to the programme, we collect your name, email, and (optionally) phone number to place you with a coordinator and send you programme updates.

How we use it: solely to run the programme — matching you with a coordinator, sharing call sheets and materials, and sending announcements by email or SMS if you opt in.

Who sees it: your coordinator, the Local Ambassador, IChC, and no one else. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

For students under 18: if you're applying as a minor, we recommend a parent or guardian be aware of your participation and have visibility into your coordinator's contact details. Parents or guardians can reach the Ambassador at any time with questions about how their child's information is handled.

Your choices: you can opt out of email or SMS notifications at any time, and can ask the Ambassador to remove your record from the programme at any time by emailing eseyram@amb.intchc.org.
